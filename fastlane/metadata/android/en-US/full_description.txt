A turn-based chess knight strategy game. Play solo in Knight's Tour mode or challenge a friend in two-player mode on a shrinking board.

GAME MODES
• Knight's Tour (Single-player): Navigate the board and collect all tiles without repeating moves.
• Two-player: Compete against a friend to outmaneuver each other on a shrinking board.

HOW TO PLAY
Each knight moves in an L-shape, just like in chess. When you move, the tile you leave behind falls into the abyss and becomes void, so the board shrinks with every move!

TWO-PLAYER OBJECTIVE
Capture your opponent's knight, or trap them so they have no valid moves.

TWO-PLAYER RULES
Player 1 starts at the top-left corner, Player 2 at the bottom-right. Player 1 always moves first. You cannot land on void (fallen) tiles.

WIN CONDITIONS
1. Capture: land on your opponent's tile.
2. Trap: leave your opponent with no valid moves.

On a 4x4 board, Player 2 cannot capture on their first move (this balances the smaller board). If only 2 tiles remain, one under each player, the game ends in a tie.

FEATURES
- Single-player and local two-player modes on one device.
- Board sizes from 4x4 to 12x12.
- Choose your knight colors.
- No ads, no tracking, and no internet permission required.

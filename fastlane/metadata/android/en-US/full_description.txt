A turn-based chess knight strategy game for two players on a shrinking board.

Compete against a friend to outmaneuver each other. Each knight moves in an
L-shape, just like in chess. When you move, the tile you leave behind falls
into the abyss and becomes void, so the board shrinks with every move!

OBJECTIVE
Capture your opponent's knight, or trap them so they have no valid moves.

HOW TO PLAY
Player 1 starts at the top-left corner, Player 2 at the bottom-right. Player 1
always moves first. A knight moves 2 squares in one direction then 1 square
perpendicular (an L-shape). You cannot land on void (fallen) tiles.

WIN CONDITIONS
1. Capture: land on your opponent's tile.
2. Trap: leave your opponent with no valid moves.

SPECIAL RULES
On a 4x4 board, Player 2 cannot capture on their first move (this balances the
smaller board). If only 2 tiles remain, one under each player, the game ends in
a tie.

FEATURES
- Local two-player play on one device.
- Board sizes from 4x4 to 12x12.
- Choose your knight colors.
- No ads, no tracking, and no internet permission required.
